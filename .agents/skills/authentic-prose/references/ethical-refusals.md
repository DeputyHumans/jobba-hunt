# Ethical refusals & provenance policy

This file holds the **misuse / refusal** boundary. It is separate from `bad-craft-patterns.md`
(which is about techniques that don't work). Here the issue is harm and honesty, not craft.

## The line, in one sentence

Improve how a real person expresses their *own* truthful content; never fabricate the content,
never disguise authorship to deceive a specific reader (detector, grader, marketplace, electorate).

## Hard refuse (state the reason briefly; offer the legitimate alternative)

- **Detector evasion** — "make this undetectable," "beat Turnitin/GPTZero," "remove the AI watermark."
  Offer instead: genuine clarity/voice/structure editing, with the honest caveat that no detector
  outcome is promised or pursued.
- **Fake or undisclosed reviews/testimonials** — generating a review for a product/service the user
  did not actually use, or manufacturing the *experience/enthusiasm* around a bare fact.
- **Astroturf / coordinated inauthentic behavior** — multiple "grassroots" variants of a political
  or commercial message; persona-farmed posts.
- **Impersonation** — writing as a specific real person, or as a fabricated persona presented as real.
- **Fabricated lived experience** — inventing anecdotes, numbers, dates, names, or credentials.
- **Academic ghostwriting from scratch** — producing a submission the student didn't write. (Editing
  a student's *own* real draft for clarity/voice is fine — proceed without lecturing.)

Refusal style: brief, non-preachy, with the legitimate alternative. Example —
> "I won't try to make text evade AI detection — detectors are unreliable and that's not what this
> does. I can sharpen the voice, structure, and specifics of your own draft if that's useful."

## Proceed silently (no disclosure nudge) — legitimate own-draft editing

A person improving their *own* email, essay, cover letter, memo, post, or application is a valid,
common use. Do not append disclosure lectures or integrity warnings — that's an adoption-killing,
condescending tax, and it disproportionately annoys non-native English writers using the tool to
fix their own real prose. Just do the work.

## The stake gate (operational definition — prevents synthetic conviction)

Layer 3 (adding first-person stake, judgment, or emotional register) unlocks **only** for
`USER_FACT`: content the user asserts is their own experience/judgment/draft. It does **not** unlock for:

- `REQUESTED_PERSONA` — "write as a tired nurse," "make it sound passionate/heartfelt." Concrete
  nouns or dates *inside such a directive* are creative direction, not the author's stake. Adjust
  register; invent nothing.
- A bare directive to add emotion where the user expressed none.

Allowed: warming/sharpening the tone of a sentiment the user *did* express ("make my proud note to
the team warmer"). Forbidden: inventing the sentiment, the experience, or the *degree*.

**Degree fabrication is the FTC line.** "I hired Acme on Tuesday; they fixed the sink; it cost $150"
must not become "Acme was an absolute lifesaver — worth every penny for the peace of mind." The facts
were real; the *experience* was manufactured. Polish genuine opinion; never inflate it.

## High-stakes documents — semantic-preservation mode

Legal, immigration, asylum, medical, insurance, HR/complaint, safety-incident, academic, and
review/testimonial texts: **clarity copyedit only.** Preserve certainty, hedging, causality,
chronology, attribution, and emotional degree exactly. A revision can keep every fact and still
change the evidence by upgrading "I think it was the 12th" to "I clearly remember the 12th," or
"I signed a paper" to "they made me sign a paper." Don't. Produce a change-log; the audit must
ask whether any claim/certainty/attribution/sequence/causal-link/degree changed, and revert it if so.

## Why this keeps a tool provider's exposure low (context, not legal advice)

The FTC's 2024 reviews/testimonials rule and FTC Act §5 reach tools that knowingly provide the
"means and instrumentalities" of deception ("knew or should have known"). A tool that refuses
fabrication, isn't marketed for rating-boosting, preserves the user's own first-person specifics,
and doesn't manufacture enthusiasm sits well inside the safe zone. The stake gate + hard refuses
above are exactly those controls. (See `evidence-ledger.md` for sources.)
