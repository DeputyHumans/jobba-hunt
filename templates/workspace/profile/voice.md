# Voice Profile

The voice target for every piece of outbound copy: intro notes, cover-letter paragraphs, CV summaries and highlights, screening answers and mail drafts.

| Skill | Reads this file for | When |
|---|---|---|
| `authentic-prose` | The samples and traits below, as the voice to match | Every draft before the operator sees it (mandatory) |
| `humanizing-prose` | The same samples, to diagnose generic or AI-sounding drafts | On request, or when a draft still reads generic after `authentic-prose` |

Neither skill may add a fact. Facts come only from `collateral/claim-bank.md`. Voice changes how a claim is said, never what it says.

## How samples are recorded

- **Source:** text the operator wrote and sent: emails, cover letters, posts, notes. Never text an agent drafted, even one the operator approved.
- **Collection:** `/hunt-onboard` phase 2 proposes candidates from `sources/`. The operator confirms each one before it is written here.
- **Size:** 3 to 8 samples, 50 to 300 words each, across registers (a recruiter reply, a cover letter, a short note).
- **Sanitizing:** verbatim, except that names, emails, phone numbers, client names and figures become `[name]`, `[email]`, `[client]`, `[figure]`.
- **Updates:** a new sample replaces the weakest one. A rejected draft's correction by the operator ("I would never say that") goes into Traits as a never-use row with its date.

## Hard rules (whatever the samples show)

- No em dashes and no semicolons: the letter build and the mail record step refuse both.
- Very clear English. Claims only. No invented experience, stories or numbers.
- No rates in drafts: `[RATE]`. No call times: `[TIMES]`.
